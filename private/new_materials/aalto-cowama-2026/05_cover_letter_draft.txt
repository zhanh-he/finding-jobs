Dear Professor Juvela and the selection committee,

I am applying for the Postdoctoral Researcher position in Speech and Language Technology on COWAMA. My doctoral research at the University of Western Australia connects audio representations, musical expression and deep learning. I have completed my doctoral defence and expect my graduation certificate in November 2026. The position's explicit openness to researchers close to completing their doctorate fits this stage of my work.

COWAMA's focus on content-based watermarking is especially interesting to me because it combines generative audio with a difficult evaluation question: which changes preserve useful information while remaining perceptually acceptable? My research on differentiable SoundFont proxies uses loudness-related supervision to adapt MIDI velocity estimation across instruments. My related work includes reimplementing a Bark-scale specific-loudness feature extractor in PyTorch and developing models linking musical dynamics with beat and structural information. These experiences would help me contribute to perceptual and content-aware evaluation while learning the project's watermarking methods.

At Dolby, I developed music-generation models using U-Net, VAE and masked Transformer architectures and designed professional listening tests. My later research internship at Huawei concerns music-model post-training and human-preference evaluation. I would bring this combination of generative modelling and listening-based assessment to experiments that distinguish watermark detectability, audio quality and robustness under transformations.

I would begin by reproducing an agreed baseline, defining a transparent attack and quality-evaluation protocol, and examining where content-aware representations offer robustness beyond local signal cues. Watermarking, neural audio codecs and diffusion-model integration would be research areas for me to develop, rather than prior specialisms I claim to possess. I am motivated by COWAMA's commitment to open software, trained models and reproducible outputs, subject to data and licence constraints.

My teaching and Honours project co-supervision at UWA have also prepared me to work constructively with the project's doctoral researcher. I would welcome the opportunity to contribute to the Speech Synthesis group's international collaboration and to develop a rigorous programme of research on generated-audio provenance.

Thank you for considering my application.
