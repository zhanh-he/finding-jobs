SELECTED RESEARCH
Perceptually informed cross-instrument modelling
- Contributed to differentiable SoundFont proxies to supervise MIDI velocity from rendered-audio loudness and adapt a piano-pretrained estimator to other instruments.
- Studied supervision that reduces reliance on scarce non-piano velocity labels.
Psychoacoustic features and multitask learning
- Contributed to reimplementing a Bark-scale specific-loudness extractor in PyTorch.
- Contributed to a multitask CRNN for piano dynamics, beat/downbeat and structural-change estimation.
Generation and subjective evaluation
- Contributed to adapting a U-Net image-colourisation approach to per-note MIDI velocity generation; designed a listening study supported by Dolby professional listeners.
Score-informed model refinement
- Contributed to a Transformer correction module using score onset and frame information to refine velocities from piano transcription models.
Project repository: https://github.com/zhanh-he/sfproxy-velocity-estimation
