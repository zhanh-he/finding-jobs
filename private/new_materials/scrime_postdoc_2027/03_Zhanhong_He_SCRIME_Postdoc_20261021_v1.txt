RESEARCH PROFILE

SELECTED RESEARCH
Cross-instrument musical dynamics
- Contributed to a differentiable SoundFont proxy approach for adapting a piano-pretrained velocity estimator to other instruments using audio-loudness supervision.
- Investigated expressive rendering where reliable non-piano velocity labels are scarce.
Score-informed transcription refinement
- Contributed to a Transformer correction approach that uses score onset and frame information to refine MIDI velocities produced by piano transcription models.
Joint dynamics and musical structure
- Contributed to a multitask CRNN for dynamics, beats, downbeats and structural changes, and to reimplementing a Bark-scale specific-loudness extractor in PyTorch.
MIDI expression generation and evaluation
- Contributed to adapting an image-colourisation U-Net to fill per-note MIDI velocities; designed a listening study conducted with support from Dolby professional listeners.
Project repository: https://github.com/zhanh-he/sfproxy-velocity-estimation
