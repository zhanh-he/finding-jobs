SELECTED RESEARCH
Expressive dynamics across instruments
• Developed research on adapting a piano-pretrained MIDI velocity estimator to other instruments using differentiable SoundFont proxies and audio-loudness supervision.
• Investigated supervision that reduces reliance on note-level velocity labels in non-piano recordings.

Score-informed velocity refinement
• Developed a Transformer correction approach using score onset and frame information to refine velocity estimates from piano transcription models.

Joint dynamics and musical structure modelling
• Developed a multitask CRNN for piano dynamic markings, beats, downbeats and structural changes.
• Reimplemented a Bark-scale specific-loudness feature extractor in PyTorch for musical audio analysis.

MIDI expression generation and listening evaluation
• Adapted a U-Net image-colourisation approach to predict per-note MIDI velocity.
• Designed a listening test conducted with support from professional listeners at Dolby.
